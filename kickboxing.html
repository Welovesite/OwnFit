What we do

What your sessions cover

Every session is one-to-one and 60 minutes, at a place you choose.

Punching basics

Clean, balanced punches as the base of every combination.

Kicking technique

Front, roundhouse and side kicks with proper hip turn.

Combinations

Mix hands and kicks into smooth, powerful sequences.

Defence

Blocks, checks and movement to stay safe.

Balance & flexibility

Hip mobility and stability for higher, stronger kicks.

Conditioning rounds

High-intensity intervals that burn calories fast.

Who it is for

Made for every level

  • Beginners looking for an exciting workout
  • People bored of regular gym routines
  • Martial artists returning to training
  • Anyone who wants power and cardio together
What you need

Your own gloves (hand wraps recommended), a clear space, water and a towel. If you train in a building, compound or gym, please arrange your coach's entry in advance.

A typical session

How a session runs

  1. Warm-upMobility, skipping and dynamic stretches.
  2. Technique drillsPunches, kicks and defence, step by step.
  3. Combination roundsTimed rounds putting it all together.
  4. Core & cool-downCore strength, stretching and review.
