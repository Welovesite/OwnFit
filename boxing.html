Boxing

Learn to box properly or use boxing to get fit. Technique, footwork and fight fitness, one-to-one with a boxing coach.

Book boxing Meet the coaches
What we do

What your sessions cover

Every session is one-to-one and 60 minutes, at a place you choose.

Stance & guard

Build a solid base and keep your hands in the right place.

Footwork

Move in, out and around with balance and control.

Punch technique

Jab, cross, hooks and uppercuts thrown with correct form.

Combinations

Link punches together with speed and rhythm.

Defence

Slips, rolls, blocks and parries to avoid getting hit.

Fight fitness

Timed rounds that build boxing-specific stamina.

Who it is for

Made for every level

  • Complete beginners
  • People who want a fun way to get fit
  • Boxers sharpening their technique
  • Anyone building confidence and self-defence skills
What you need

Your own boxing gloves (hand wraps recommended), a clear space, water and a towel. If you train in a building, compound or gym, please arrange your coach's entry in advance.

A typical session

How a session runs

  1. Warm-upSkipping, shadow boxing and mobility.
  2. Technique drillsOne skill at a time, broken down by your coach.
  3. RoundsPut the skills together in timed rounds.
  4. Conditioning & cool-downCore work, stretching and session review.
