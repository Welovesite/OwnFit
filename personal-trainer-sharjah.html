Personal training in Sharjah: questions

How much does a personal trainer cost in Sharjah?

OwnFit packages are AED 3,500 for 12 sessions (≈ AED 292 per session), AED 4,200 for 16 sessions (≈ AED 263) and AED 6,000 for 24 sessions (AED 250). Each session is 60 minutes, one-to-one.

Do you come to my home in Sharjah?

Yes. Your coach comes to your home, building gym, rooftop, a park, the beach or your office anywhere in Sharjah. You arrange access if your building or gym needs a visitor pass.

Do I need my own equipment?

Yes. OwnFit provides the coach only. Please have your own gloves for boxing, kickboxing and Muay Thai, plus water, a towel and space to train.

Is there a female personal trainer in Sharjah?

Yes. Choose Coach Fatima, or pick "Female coach" in the booking form, and we will match you.

I am a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Most clients start from zero. Your coach builds the plan around your level and pace.
