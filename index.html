FAQ

Before your first session

Do I need any equipment?

Yes. OwnFit provides the coach only, not equipment or facilities. Please have your own equipment ready, such as gloves for boxing, kickboxing and Muay Thai, plus water, a towel and enough clear space to train.

Where can we train?

Anywhere you are allowed to train: your home or villa, your building gym or rooftop, a park, the beach or your office.

Who arranges access to my building or gym?

You do. If your building, compound or gym needs a visitor pass, guest fee or security approval, please arrange it before the session so your coach can get in on time.

Can I ask for a female coach?

Yes. Choose your preferred coach when you book and we will match you.

I have never boxed before. Is that okay?

Yes. Most clients start as complete beginners. Your coach starts with stance and basics and builds up at your pace.

Can I train with my partner or friends?

Yes. The Crew package covers small groups at one location, including families and office teams.
