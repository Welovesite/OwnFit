Muay Thai

The art of eight limbs. Punches, kicks, elbows, knees and the clinch, taught one-to-one by Muay Thai coaches.

What we do

What your sessions cover

Every session is one-to-one and 60 minutes, at a place you choose.

Thai stance & rhythm

The upright stance and timing that make Muay Thai unique.

Kicks & teeps

Roundhouse kicks and push kicks with full power.

Elbows & knees

Close-range strikes thrown safely and correctly.

Clinch work

Control, off-balance and strike from the clinch.

Combinations

Link all eight weapons into fluid attacks.

Thai conditioning

The demanding conditioning Muay Thai is known for.

Who it is for

Made for every level

  • Beginners curious about Muay Thai
  • Fitness lovers who want a real challenge
  • Fighters preparing for a camp or bout
  • Anyone building strength, discipline and confidence
What you need

Your own gloves (hand wraps recommended), a clear space, water and a towel. If you train in a building, compound or gym, please arrange your coach's entry in advance.

A typical session

How a session runs

  1. Warm-upSkipping, shadow work and joint mobility.
  2. TechniqueStrikes, defence and footwork, one skill at a time.
  3. RoundsTimed rounds combining everything you learned.
  4. Conditioning & cool-downCore, clinch strength and stretching.
